# P8 overlap and weighting

Strict P8 weighting status: unsupported. Required support is at least five independent positive hosts per cell, maximum weight five and effective sample size at least ten. No weights are assigned to unsupported regions; ESS is unavailable, not a fabricated zero-variance estimate.

The original sampling identifier `p8_proxy` is retained for auditability. Its two-minute-coverage parser was faulty; the corrected inventory shows that it is a cool/faint stellar oversample, not a representative P8 FFI sample.
