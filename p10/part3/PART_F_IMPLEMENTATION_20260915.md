## Adopted methods [A]

The original allocation remains all 436 eligible stars for both presets, with six pilot hosts reused. Part F verifies the original/execution registration chain, both final prediction locks, the closed early-funnel evidence, the Part D native-noise records and the sidecar hash. It records the entry boundary and marks all original 531 stars development-exposed before parsing the sidecar. The prepared input for a forced fit must have the same SHA-256 as both searches' frozen input for that host.

Rows use source filename and source row as their identity. Families use the registered ascending-period, strict 1% anchor grouping without transitive chaining. The highest finite model-SNR member, then source filename and row number, supplies the canonical family ephemeris for one forced fit. Within each independence stratum, the same deterministic ranking supplies that stratum's model SNR, expected SNR and period bin; the union uses the canonical representative. Family domain and likely-periodic membership are the logical OR over members of the relevant stratum. A likely-periodic in-domain family requires at least one member satisfying both conditions. Mixed domain membership is explicitly recorded. Recovery is the logical OR over in-domain member matches. These representative and membership choices do not depend on recovery or presence outcomes. Raw-row tables inherit their family's single forced-presence measurement and do not imply independent fits for duplicate rows.

For each accepted signal, the nearest of period ratios 1, 2, 3, 1/2 and 1/3 is eligible only at strictly less than 1% relative error. Only ratio 1 receives exact-match credit. Epoch checks use the registered strict uncertainty condition, sigma_P times absolute cycles from TCE epoch to the prepared-data midpoint less than T14/2. Otherwise the row is explicitly period-only. For a checkable row, propagate the TCE epoch to its nearest event at the midpoint and the accepted epoch to its nearest event there. Agreement is at most T14/2 modulo the common fundamental period P_TCE times min(1, alias ratio). This permits an integer-period alias to align with the corresponding TCE events while preserving its alias label. It adds no 3:2 or 2:3 ratios. Missing accepted epochs fail a required epoch check. TCE time0 errors are retained but do not alter the registered period-error criterion.

Expected SNR is (TCE depth in ppm times 1e-6 / Part D sigma) times sqrt(actual prepared cadence count times duration in hours / 24 / period in days). Missing or nonphysical inputs are unmeasured. Expected-SNR bin edges remain 0, 7, 10, 15, 20 and 30; model-SNR edges remain 9, 12 and 20. Search domain remains inclusive 0.5–30 days. Outside-domain rows and families are reported separately and never called misses.

Each evaluated family, including outside-domain families, receives exactly one fixed-period, fixed-duration two-level box fit on its frozen prepared time/flux arrays. Duration is max(TCE T14, the measured Part D median native cadence). There is no additional detrending, clipping, weighting or period search. When the canonical representative's epoch is checkable, that epoch fixes the box phase. Otherwise the algorithm enumerates every circular phase-window partition using entry/exit boundaries and intervening intervals, chooses the greatest positive-depth least-squares improvement, and breaks ties at the first sorted phase. The box is half-open at its right edge. Fitted depth is mean(outside) minus mean(inside). Its standard error is sqrt((residual SSE / (N-2)) times (1/N_inside + 1/N_outside)).

Presence requires positive fitted depth at least three finite positive standard errors. A smaller measured depth is absent. A nonfinite/zero standard error, insufficient in/out samples, invalid ephemeris, timeout or other failed fit is operationally unknown. This error model assumes common independent residual variance and is conditional on the selected phase; it has no phase-trials correction or correlated-noise inflation. Consequently, especially for best-phase fits, the registered presence label is a diagnostic and is not a calibrated global false-alarm probability. This limitation must accompany the results; it does not change the registered three-sigma rule.

Forced fits use at most 24 top-level workers, single-thread numerical libraries, a 300-second deadline and one scientific attempt per family. Controller v4 preserves the owning-controller pattern and adds a durable integrity-failure signal checked before launch and before every dispatch loop. On abort, terminated attempts are removed from its active map before the closure receipt is written. Runtime, source, prediction, method, entry, noise and native-input hashes are checked before the fit. A hash failure halts the queue; ordinary fit failures remain unknown. No scientific retry is permitted.

All fractions use known, measured denominators with unknown and outside-domain counts alongside them. Empty denominators are unmeasured, never zero. Generate 10,000 whole-host resamples with PCG64 seed 2026091401 in the frozen evaluated-host order, reusing each host multiplicity vector across both presets, strata, rows and families. Retention is not conditioned on absent signals; present and missed is reported distinctly. Sequential funnel retention uses prior-stage passers as the conditional denominator and present in-domain signals as the cumulative population. Multiple matched candidates are combined by logical OR: any pass establishes survival; if none passes and any applicable result is unknown, the family remains unknown. Stage S PASS or HOLD is carried to B. C not run due to the cap is unmeasured and is not a rejection.
