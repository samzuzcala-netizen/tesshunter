# AMENDMENT_K1_20261008_2 — exposure-aware Stage A per-event depth

Dated 2026-10-08 (UTC).  Written after the C4 timing pilot and before any Part D run: no injection,
false-alarm, adversarial or known-planet campaign has been run, no rule E1-E4 outcome other than the
mechanical E4 budget has been computed, and no HELD-OUT set has been touched.

**Change.**  Definition 1.5 (Stage A: "p10.0 event rules and six criteria; K1 cadence-aware counts")
gains one clause: in every K1 funnel (both arms) the per-event depth on the detrended series that
feeds the six criteria is the least-squares amplitude of a limb-darkened chord template integrated
over the observing product's exposure (`event_vet.EVENT_DEPTH_TEMPLATE = "exposure_ld"`; quadratic
coefficients 0.45 / 0.20, impact parameter 0, 15 sub-samples, unit chord mean), fitted to every
cadence the template overlaps (|t - tc| < T/2 + exposure/2), with error = point scatter /
sqrt(sum template^2).  The six criteria, their thresholds, the event removal rules, the red-noise
beta inflation and the p-value the gate reads are unchanged.  Pre-K1 rule versions are unchanged
(the switch defaults to None = the box mean inside +/- T/2).

**Why (implementation defect, not a threshold change).**  [M] In the C4 pilot (40 injection hosts,
one injection each, `survey_k1` at 8,100 s), Stage A killed 11 of the 26 injections that reached the
pre-gate; 10 of those 11 failed only `depths_consistent_p_ge_1e-3`, at stitch SNR 12-485.  The
`survey_k1_s56` arm (single 200-s cadence) passed the same injections.  [M] On one of them
(TIC 293650295, P 3.10 d, T 0.060 d) the box-mean depths by exposure were 14.47 (200 s, n 35),
14.32 (600 s, n 21) and 13.48 ppt (1800 s, n 14), with within-class chi2/dof 1.4, 3.5 and 12.3:
the box mean of a coarse-cadence event is smeared shallow and depends on where the few exposures
fall relative to ingress.  The box mean is a cadence statistic that section B1 of the prompt
requires to be exposure-aware ("Stage A kept-event SNR"); it was missed in Part B.  An
exposure-integrated limb-darkened template brought the class means of that case to 13.30 / 13.36 /
13.29 ppt and the 1800-s within-class chi2/dof to 3.2.  [M] Synthetic three-cadence test
(`k1/tests/test_k1_event_depth.py`, template coefficients deliberately different from the injected
ones, b = 0.4): box mean chi2/dof 17-30 (p < 1e-80); template chi2/dof 1.1-1.3 (p 0.12-0.29); an
alternating-depth (8 / 5 ppt) control still fails (p < 1e-3, odd-even > 5 sigma).

**Effect.**  The C4 pilot's Stage A (and therefore S, B and the price) is re-measured with the
amendment under labels `pilot2` / `pilot2_s56`; the first pilot's receipts stay on disk as the
record of the defect.  Part D and everything after it run with the amendment.

**Choice made here that is not mechanical [I].**  The template shape (generic K-dwarf TESS-band
limb darkening, b = 0) is a modelling choice; the synthetic test shows the consistency statistic is
insensitive to a mismatch of that size.  This amendment is reported to the user with the STOP-C
pause and can be withdrawn before Part D.
