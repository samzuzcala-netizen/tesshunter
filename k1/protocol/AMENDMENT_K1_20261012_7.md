# AMENDMENT_K1_20261012_7: the candidate period in the identity rule (evaluator defect)

Dated 2026-10-12 (UTC). This is an evaluator-side fix. Search, funnel and decision thresholds are
unchanged, and no prediction changes. It was written before the Part F prediction lock, with the HELD-OUT
truth still sealed. Part D results had been seen: the defect was found in the Part D calibration
analysis.

**Defect [M].** Rule 2.2 matches a candidate (P_c, T0_c, d_c) to a truth signal by event coincidence on
the arm's timestamps. The evaluator took P_c from the candidate's reported `period_days`. For candidates
found in one chunk, that is the chunk's BLS period.

At expected SNR >= 40, 119 of 318 survey_k1 injections were scored "not found at search". All 119 had a
candidate within 1% of the injected period. Their chunk periods were off by 5e-5 to 1.7e-4 (relative).
Over the ~2,600-day all-sector baseline (up to ~2,400 cycles) the predicted events drift off the
transits, leaving coverage 0.26-0.68, below the 0.8 required.

With the candidate's stitch re-measured period (`stitch_period_days`, measured by the pipeline on all of
the arm's data; relative error ~1e-6), 117 of the 119 match (coverage ~1.0).

**Change.** P_c = `stitch_period_days` when it is finite and within 1% of `period_days`; otherwise
`period_days` (`match_k1.candidate_period`). This applies to:

* every ledger (`evaluate_k1.ledger_item`);
* the HELD-OUT and adversarial matching (`evaluate_partF_k1`, amendments 4-5);
* the C-sample planner (already run; its selection used the old P_c, noted).

T0_c, d_c and every tolerance are unchanged. A unit test covers the helper and the drift
(`k1/tests/test_k1_match.py`).

**Effect.** Completeness at every stage was understated, more so on the longer all-sector baseline than
on survey_k1_s56. The Part D tables, E1-E3 retentions and the SNR50s are recomputed under this
amendment and reported next to the pre-fix values. The frozen decisions (survey_k1, bound_alias, legacy
ramp, 8,100 s) are re-checked: if any rule outcome changes, the change is reported before Part F is
scored.

The C sample (amendment 6) was drawn with the old matcher, so injections whose only S-passing path had a
drifting chunk period were not selectable. This is noted as a selection effect on C | S.
