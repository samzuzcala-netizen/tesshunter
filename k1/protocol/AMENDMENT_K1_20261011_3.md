# AMENDMENT_K1_20261011_3: E1 evaluated under the S mode E2 selects (POST-HOC)

Dated 2026-10-11 (UTC). **Post-hoc.** It was written after E1 had been applied as registered and after
its S-mode sensitivity had been measured (`k1/results/E1_DECISION_K1.md`). The user chose it in chat
over keeping E1 as registered. No HELD-OUT set has been touched, and E2's adversarial pass-through
(DEV Stage D) had not been measured when this was written.

**Registered E1 outcome (kept on record).** Under S mode `bound`, survey_k1 minus survey_k1_s56 =
+2.70 pp (host-bootstrap 95% [-2.56, +7.78]) on 259 pool injections, so (a) fails; (b) 1.22 and
(c) 1.69 hold. The registered rule adopts survey_k1_s56.

**Change.** Sections 3 E1 and E2 are applied in this order. Every condition and threshold is
unchanged; only the S mode under which E1's retention is measured changes.

1. Apply E2 exactly as registered with survey_k1 as the arm. The pool is survey_k1 injections at
   expected SNR 7-40, plus DEV known planets with >= 3 observed transits. The adversarial
   constraint uses the DEV pass-through at the end of D. Call the selected mode m.
2. Apply E1's conditions (a)-(c) exactly as registered, but with retention through S under mode m
   in both arms (pool: injections at expected SNR 7-20 on the all-sector data; host bootstrap as
   before). If m is `bound`, this is the registered E1.
3. If E1 adopts survey_k1, the frozen configuration is survey_k1 with S mode m. Otherwise
   survey_k1_s56 is adopted, and E2 is applied on the survey_k1_s56 arm as registered.

The injection C sample is drawn from the adopted arm under the frozen mode or `bound`, as in
`plan_partD_C_k1.py`, after step 3.

**Why.** [M] The all-sector arm leads by +10.8 pp through Stage A ([+5.3, +16.4]). Its lead shrinks to
+2.7 pp only under the S modes without an off-phase rescue (`bound`, `empirical_bound`). It is
+9.7 pp ([+3.9, +15.2]) under `bound_alias` and +6.9 pp ([+1.2, +12.8]) under the recurrent modes.

[I] The survey runs with the mode E2 selects. Comparing the arms under a different mode than the one
that will be used makes the cadence decision depend on an ordering choice made at registration,
not on the survey's actual funnel.

**Risk stated.** This is a post-hoc change made after seeing outcomes (a forking-paths risk). It is
mitigated because:

* the mode is fixed by E2's own registered rule, with its adversarial constraint, not chosen for the
  E1 result;
* every threshold is unchanged;
* Part F's HELD-OUT gate (G1-G3, including the paired non-inferiority test against P12) is the
  independent check of the result.
