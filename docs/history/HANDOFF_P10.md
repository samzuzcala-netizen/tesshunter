D_ref is the reference depth defined in 0.1.1; `sigma_s` is the net box error of that sector. The factor 0.5 is a fixed dilution allowance for the one-pixel aperture (see 0.11).
  - A sector with no usable pixels, fewer than the required 16 sky pixels, a zero-error entry, or `0.5 x D_ref < 3 x sigma_s` is unmeasurable and counts for neither clause. Missing pixel evidence is never "no contamination".
- **K8.** The physical fit implies a stellar companion: the adopted fit's radius exceeds 1.5 Jupiter radii (16.81 Earth radii) at the adopted stellar radius; or the density prior is rejected above 3 sigma (`delta_chi2_prior_minus_free >= 9`) with a converged fit (`uncertainty_issues` empty), a non-grazing geometry (`b + rp_rs < 1`), and a fitted-over-catalogue density ratio outside [0.5, 2] (the factor-2 guard of 0.11). A grazing-rail fit is H2, not K8.
- **K9.** Artefact-carried under D review. The one human judgment in the tiers, with a numeric criterion that must hold before it may be applied and whose numbers the reviewer records on the record. Both clauses, each in at least two sectors with a target-aperture record:
  - K9a: the Pearson correlation between the raw one-pixel target-aperture series and the sky series (both as saved by `p8_target_aperture.py`, normalised to the net median) over the cadences within 3 fit durations of every predicted event centre in that sector satisfies `|r| > 0.7`;
  - K9b: the sky-subtracted (net) fixed-aperture depth disagrees with the reference depth: `d_s > D_ref + 3 x sigma_tot`, or `d_s < 0.5 x D_ref - 3 x sigma_tot`, or `d_s < -3 x sigma_s`, where `sigma_tot = sqrt(sigma_s^2 + sigma_ref^2)`.
  - The reviewer may not apply K9 when the criterion does not hold, and is not obliged to apply it when it does; either way the numbers and the decision are on the record. A candidate without target-aperture records in at least two sectors cannot be K9.

**Otherwise HOLD when any of these holds:**

- **H1.** TRICERATOPS NFPP >= 0.5 on the binned fold. A `fpp_uniform` result (failed likelihood) is an unavailable measurement: it does not trigger H1 and is reported in F4.
- **H2.** The all-transit fit is unconverged (`uncertainty_issues` non-empty) or on the grazing rail with unusable geometry uncertainties: `adopted.grazing` is true and (`b_err > 0.5` or `t14_err_hours >= t14_hours`).
- **H3.** A D measurement (the fit, the FPP, the centroid, the difference image or the target aperture) did not execute, or errored, for a cause that a re-run can remove (a download failure, a timeout, a missing input that exists). Operational; every H3 must be cleared (re-run) before the final list. An H3 that cannot be cleared is reported as such and the candidate stays HOLD.
- **H4.** A VSX entry within 21 arcsec of the target carries class EA, EB, EW or ELL and the candidate period matches none of its aliases (a match is K5). Other VSX classes (ROT and the rest) are flags, not HOLDs.

**Otherwise SUBMIT**, always carrying the flag list F1 to F9 on the record, in the packet and in `ctoi_fields.json`:

- F1 every neighbour within 63 arcsec that could produce the depth by total eclipse, with the dilution factor each would need (the existing Gaia depth-context calculation as a list);
- F2 Gaia RUWE, `ipd_frac_multi_peak`, excess-noise significance, `non_single_star`, variability flag;
- F3 VSX and SIMBAD entries within 1 arcmin with class and separation;
- F4 FPP and NFPP with their caveats (binned fold, circular aperture, first three sectors, scenario-conditional; `fpp_uniform` reported as unavailable);
- F5 pixel localisation: on target, unlocalised, or not measurable, with the per-sector fixed-aperture depth and error for every reviewed sector and the centroid significance where measured;
- F6 other accepted signals on the host;
- F7 SPOC TCE, TOI or CTOI association (Part 3g);
- F8 the artefact-class census of the kept events (the eight classes);
- F9 `validation_status`: the specific external evidence that would settle the source.

The ordered submission list ranks SUBMITs by `ranking.score_v2`. No verdict depends on rank. For controls, "SUBMIT-equivalent" means the same tiers applied to the control's blind candidate exactly as P9 defined it (the known-status hard stop redacted, `actual_submission_eligible` false).

### 0.2 Part 2a: the secondary scan [A, Y = 90%]

### 0.11 Changes to the prompt's defaults, made before any measurement

1. **K7b absence test.** The prompt's default reads "the target aperture shows no dimming at 3 sigma when the fitted depth is at least 3 times the per-sector error". Taken literally, a sector whose expected dip is 3.0 sigma and whose measured dip is 2.9 sigma would count as absence. The adopted form requires the measurement to exclude the (diluted) reference depth at 3 sigma: `d_s < 0.5 x D_ref - 3 x sigma_s`, applied only where `0.5 x D_ref >= 3 x sigma_s`. Reason: an absence kill must be an exclusion, not a marginal non-detection.
2. **Dilution allowance 0.5 in K7b and K9b.** The fixed one-pixel aperture on a 21-arcsec pixel is diluted by neighbours (P8: "Fixed apertures remain diluted"). The reference depth is halved before the absence and too-shallow comparisons so that dilution alone cannot fire them. The factor is fixed at 0.5 for the whole prompt [A]; it is not fitted per star.
3. **K8 density clause guard.** The prompt's default kills on a density-prior rejection above 3 sigma with converged non-grazing geometry. The catalogue density error is an adopted 30% (P8 assumptions), not a measurement, so a precise fit can reject the prior at 3 sigma with a small ratio. The adopted form adds a factor-2 guard: the fitted-over-catalogue density ratio must also lie outside [0.5, 2]. Reason: a stellar companion implies a large mismatch, not a marginal one.
4. **K9 made computable.** The prompt gives K9's criterion in words; 0.1 K9 states the exact series, window (3 fit durations about every event centre), correlation (Pearson), and the three disagreement forms, so the reviewer's numbers can be recomputed. No threshold was changed (|r| > 0.7, 3 sigma, two sectors).

No other default was changed. Y = 90% (2a), the 1.5 x FA_pre and 3 x wall bounds and the 5-percentage-point floor (2b), the 0.5 concentration trigger and SDE 9 (2c i), the 35 of 37 agreement rule (2c iii), 14.0 and 150 (stop rule 1), 30% (3f), 1 of N (stop rule 2) and 0 of 18 (stop rule 3) are the prompt's values.
