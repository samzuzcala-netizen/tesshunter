# Survey population: where to look, chosen by a yield model

Generated by `scripts/write_population_memo.py` from `data/survey/yield_model_v1.json`
(itself written by `scripts/build_survey_population.py`, seed 20260901, 240 tiles).
Catalog versions used for the hard stop: `ctoi=2026-09-01:f6964541;nasa_ps=2026-09-01:85f3c196;nasa_pscomppars=2026-09-01:4843e49c;spoc_tce=2026-09-01:e580dde7;tess_eb=2026-09-01:43555226;toi=2026-09-01:5f54a285`.
Remediated after the P1 review (see `P1_REMEDIATION_REPORT.md`); the P1 numbers are
kept in `HANDOFF_P1.md` for comparison.

The pipeline finds candidates. Nothing in this memo is a detection.

## 1. Question and answer

Bright 2-minute targets are exhausted by SPOC and the TOI process. The
question is which full-frame-image (FFI) population gives a small pipeline
the best odds of a new, defensible candidate per star searched.

**Primary stratum: 3 (multi-sector stitched search of FFI dwarfs Tmag 10.5 to 13.5, periods to 100 d).**
**Fallback: 2 (faint M dwarfs, Tmag 13.5 to 15).**

Selection rule (version 4, fixed before the extended sample was examined; the
earlier versions are kept verbatim in the JSON): among strata 2 and 3 (1 is a subset
of 3; 4 is excluded by the hard stop), (i) the prefix-stable tile sequence is
extended until **both** candidates deliver at least 2.0 expected new signals from
the sampled stars under the central model, so that deliverability cannot decide;
(ii) the metric is the expected number of NEW detected signals per 1,000 eligible
stars at mid prior completeness under the central model (FGK occurrence from
Kunimoto & Matthews 2020, M-dwarf occurrence from Dressing & Charbonneau 2015,
missing contamination ratios imputed with the measured median); (iii) the higher
metric is primary when the ratio is at least 1.5; below that the model cannot
separate the two and the documented operational criteria decide in order:
fraction of the eligible pool with 4+ sectors of light curves, then median Tmag.

Outcome on 240 tiles: both candidates satisfy the floor (stratum 2 delivers
3.56, stratum 3 delivers 20.75; the smallest prefix on which both do is
162 tiles). Metric: stratum 2 1.451 against stratum 3
1.006 per 1,000 stars, ratio 1.44. Decided by: metric ratio inside tie_margin: secondary criteria (coverage, then brightness) decide.
Tile bootstrap (1000 draws): ratio 16th/50th/84th percentiles
1.38 / 1.44 / 1.51;
probability that stratum 2's density exceeds stratum 3's: 1.00;
probability that the ratio lies outside the tie margin: 0.20.
The sensitivity variants in section 5 do not reverse this choice.

## 2. How the population was sampled (reproducible)

* 240 sky tiles of radius 0.75 deg, drawn uniformly on the sphere with
  seed 20260901, keeping only |b| >= 20.0 deg (crowding and contamination). The
  sequence is prefix-stable: the first 120 tiles are the P1 tiles. Tile area
  1.77 deg^2 each; the union covers 420.0 deg^2 (Monte Carlo; 1.0% overlap),
  1/65 of the 27,144 deg^2 at |b| >= 20.0 deg. All-sky counts are sampled
  counts times 64.6; stars in two tiles count once (237 duplicate rows dropped).
* Every TIC star with Tmag <= 17.0 in each tile was pulled through astroquery
  (`Catalogs.query_criteria`, catalog TIC, objType STAR). The tile tables are one
  neighbour list for the 21-arcsec check; the other, complete one is the
  coordinate tree of every known object, which now includes every SPOC TCE host
  (coordinates resolved from the TIC for all 68,232 unique TCE TIC IDs,
  0 unresolved, 3,160 of them fainter than Tmag 17 and
  5 not typed STAR, which the tile query would have missed).
* Dwarfs: TIC radius <= 1.5 Rsun and Teff in [2700, 7200] K.
  M dwarfs: Teff <= 4000 K and radius <= 0.6 Rsun.
* Sectors observed per star come from tess-point (vendored, version 0.9.5 with
  the upstream missing-comma fix), capped at sector 105, the last sector with
  public data on the build date. Light-curve availability is exact: the QLP and
  TESS-SPOC per-sector target lists on MAST were streamed and intersected with
  the pool. 100.0% of stratum-1 stars have at least one QLP light curve,
  50.7% at least one TESS-SPOC light curve; median sectors with a light
  curve: 6 (stratum 1), 5 (stratum 2).

## 4. Yield model: every input

Expected detectable transiting planets per star =
sum over occurrence cells (period bin x radius bin) of
rate(cell) x P_transit(P, R*, M*) x P_detect(SNR, N_transits).

**Occurrence rates, central model.**
FGK stars (Teff > 4000 K): Kunimoto, M. & Matthews, J. M. 2020, AJ, 159, 248; arXiv:2004.05296, baseline FGK occurrence rates (Fig. 'FGK Occurrence Rates', file FGK_occurrence.png in the arXiv source);
median of the ABC posterior; uncertainty is the larger of the 15.9/84.1 percentile offsets; period edges [0.78, 1.56, 3.12, 6.25, 12.5, 25.0, 50.0, 100.0, 200.0, 400.0] d,
radius edges [0.5, 0.71, 1.0, 1.41, 2.0, 2.83, 4.0, 5.66, 8.0, 11.31, 16.0] Re; upper-limit cells taken as 0 (no detected planets; central value taken as 0, 84.1th-percentile limit kept in KM2020_FGK_UL_PERCENT).
Transcription check against the paper's own marginalized table: 2-4 Re P<50 d
25.1% (paper 25.2), 1-2 Re 16.1% (16.2), 2-4 Re P<100 d 35.3% (35.4).
M dwarfs (Teff <= 4000 K): Dressing, C. D. & Charbonneau, D. 2015, ApJ, 807, 45; arXiv:1501.01623, Table 3 'Number of Planets Per Star Versus Orbital Period (In Percentage)', first block (Huber+2014 stellar properties);
'<26.54' (1-1.5 Re, 5.5-18.2 d) set to 25.77 from the cumulative table; other limits (<0.13) set to 0; check: Table 4 cumulative rates: 1-1.5 Re 0.5-50 d = 56.25%, 1.5-2 Re = 45.95% (abstract: 0.56 and 0.46 per star). Giants above 4 Re around M dwarfs
are outside DC15 and use an adopted 0.01 per star at 0.5-50 d.
Cells are evaluated at their geometric-mean period and radius; a cell that
straddles a stratum's period limit is split uniformly in ln P.

**Legacy model (P1), kept as sensitivity:** Howard+2012 / Petigura+2013 FGK classes
(1-2 Re 0.25, 2-4 Re 0.13, 4-8 Re 0.023, 8-20 Re 0.013 per star at 1-50 d) and
DC15 abstract classes for M dwarfs (1-1.5 Re 0.56, 1.5-2 Re 0.46, 2-4 Re 0.25,
4-20 Re 0.02 at 0.5-50 d), spread with dN/dlnP proportional to min(P/10 d, 1) and
extrapolated flat in ln P beyond 50 d. Its 2-4 Re M-dwarf class (0.25) is
2.5x below the DC15 grid (0.61 at P<50 d); that is the largest single
difference between the two models.

**Noise model** (1-hour precision, ppm): photon noise from a TESS zero point of
15000 e/s at Tmag 10 (Sullivan+2015), sky of 200 e/s per pixel over an
aperture of 14 px at T=10, 8 px at T=12, 6 px at T=13.5, 4 px at T=15, 3 px at T=17 (interpolated in Tmag), a 60 ppm
systematic floor, all multiplied by an FFI-pipeline inefficiency factor of
1.5 (adopted so the curve lands on the published QLP per-30-minute scatter).
Resulting noise: T=9: 161, T=10: 240, T=10.5: 303, T=11: 389, T=12: 669, T=13: 1264, T=13.5: 1777, T=14: 2522, T=15: 5138 ppm per hour.

**Contamination.** Depth = (Rp/R*)^2 / (1 + contamination ratio). The TIC ratio is
missing for 37% of the bright dwarfs (median Tmag 13.3 against 12.4 for
measured stars) and 2% of the faint M dwarfs. The TIC computes the contamination ratio only for Candidate Target List members, and CTL membership favours isolated stars; a missing ratio therefore means 'not evaluated', not 'zero', and the measured population is if anything biased low. The central policy imputes the measured median of the same stratum (CLEAN stars); the p90 variant bounds the yield from below; zero is kept only to reproduce the P1 result.
Fill values from the CLEAN measured stars of each pool: bright dwarfs median
0.0163, 90th percentile 0.220; faint M dwarfs median 0.0146,
90th percentile 0.255. Central policy: **measured_median**. The original ratio is
kept as NaN in the target files, with `contamination_ratio_missing` and
`contamination_ratio_used` alongside.

**Detection**: duration from a central circular transit; N_transits = floor(N_sectors
x 27.4 d x 0.85 / P); SNR = depth / sigma_1h x sqrt(T14_h x N_transits); P_detect is a
logistic ramp of width 0.6 centred on the class threshold, zero below 2 transits.
Three mutually exclusive detectability classes per star (A + B + C equals the
stitched SNR >= 7.1 expectation; a unit test checks the algebra):

* **A** single-sector SNR >= 9.0 with >= 2 transits in one sector: the space QLP searches sector by sector.
* **B** stitched (all sectors with a light curve) SNR >= 9.0 and not A: the QLP multi-sector search space.
* **C** stitched 7.1 <= SNR < 9: below every FFI pipeline's release threshold.

**Prior completeness** (fraction of each class believed already caught; adopted
ranges, the single most uncertain input):

| population | class A (high/mid/low) | class B | class C | basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bright dwarfs | 0.95/0.85/0.7 | 0.8/0.6/0.4 | 0.3/0.15/0.05 | QLP has searched every Tmag<13.5 FFI star in every sector at SNR>9 (Huang+2020; Guerrero+2021) but TOI release is vetting-limited; multi-sector QLP releases exist but with lower completeness at long P; nothing releases SNR 7-9 |
| faint M dwarfs | 0.8/0.6/0.4 | 0.6/0.4/0.2 | 0.25/0.1/0.03 | QLP faint-star search (Kunimoto+2022, Tmag<15) covers part of the mission; TESS-SPOC/TGLC light curves are not systematically searched |
| TOI hosts | 0.9/0.75/0.6 | | | SPOC and QLP both run iterative multi-planet searches on every TOI host |

"high" means more already caught, so fewer left for us. **Pipeline efficiency**
(fraction of model-detectable signals a real search recovers, adopted):
A 0.8, B 0.7, C 0.4. Prompt 2 measures the real value by injection.

Expected NEW detected signals per star = sum over classes of
detectable(class) x (1 - prior completeness) x pipeline efficiency.

## 6. Choice and justification

1. Strata 1 and 3 are the same stars; 3 adds the long-baseline classes B and C
   and periods to 100 d (1.006 against 0.956 new signals per 1,000 stars).
   Searching the stitch costs no extra targets, only engine work. So 1 is never
   chosen over 3.
2. Stratum 3 versus 2 under the central model: 1.006 against
   1.451 new signals per 1,000 stars at mid priors (0.587 against 1.014 at
   high priors, 1.440 against 1.847 at low). All-sky populations
   1,333,719 against 158,345. Light-curve coverage of the clean pools: 59% of
   bright dwarfs and 36% of faint M dwarfs have 4 or more sectors with a light curve;
   median Tmag 12.8 against 14.6. The rule picks **3 (multi-sector stitched search of FFI dwarfs Tmag 10.5 to 13.5, periods to 100 d)**
   (metric ratio inside tie_margin: secondary criteria (coverage, then brightness) decide).
3. What the metric does not capture, and what the sensitivity table shows:
   faint M-dwarf signals are deeper and easier to vet photometrically, but their
   apertures are more often blended, the TIC radii and Teff at Tmag 14 to 15 are
   less reliable, and the photon-limited noise model is least trustworthy there.
   The choice between 2 and 3 moves with the FGK occurrence model (the Kunimoto &
   Matthews grid gives stratum 3 about 20% fewer new signals than the legacy
   classes) and with the faint-end noise. Prompt 2's injection test on both lists
   is what settles it; both lists are delivered.
4. Stratum 4 is excluded by the hard stop by construction (section 5).

**Target size.** At mid priors the primary needs 1,989 / 2,983 / 4,972 stars for 2 / 3 / 5
expected new signals. `targets_v1.csv` carries 819 stars: CLEAN primary-stratum stars
with at least 4 sectors of light curves, ranked by each star's own expected-new-signal
score under the central model, cut where the summed expectation reaches
5.0 (cap 20,000; 20,636 were available in the sample). Summed expected new
signals over the file: 5.00 at mid priors; 3 of its stars carry an
imputed contamination ratio. The fallback list `targets_v1_fallback.csv` holds
2,450 stars of 2 (3.56 expected new signals at mid priors).

## 7. What would change the answer

* Prior completeness for classes B and C is adopted, not measured.
* Pipeline efficiency near threshold (class C, 0.4) is the other lever; prompt 2's
  injection test replaces it with a measurement.
* The noise model is a photon model scaled to QLP's published scatter; the
  x1.4 faint-end variant in section 5 shows how much the faint stratum depends
  on it.
* The occurrence grids are transcriptions of published posteriors with
  upper-limit cells taken as zero; the DC15 2-4 Re rates are higher than later
  M-dwarf estimates and set the faint stratum's yield.
* Missing contamination ratios are imputed; the p90 variant bounds the effect.
