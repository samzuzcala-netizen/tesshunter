Generated by `scripts/analyze_completeness.py` on 2026-09-02T06:10:33Z from `data/survey/runs/inj_v1` and `data/survey/runs/fa_v1`. Config preset `survey_v1` (every field in `data/survey/completeness_v1.json`). The pipeline finds candidates; nothing here is a detection.

## 1. What was measured

* Sample: 300 stars drawn at random (seed in `completeness_sample_v1.csv`) from the 819-star primary population `targets_v1.csv`.
* Injections: 2100 batman transits (quadratic limb darkening by Teff, a/R* from the TIC mass and radius) multiplied into the cached stitched curves BEFORE detrending; period log-uniform 1-40 d in four equally populated log bins, Rp/R* log-uniform 0.01-0.12, impact parameter uniform 0-0.9, random phase. Every injection ran through the full search with the survey config.
* False alarms: the same 300 stars searched with no injection.
* Recovery rule (fixed before the run): accepted candidate with period within 1% of the injected period and overlapping transit windows. Period ratios of 2, 3, 1/2, 1/3 are `alias` (reported, not credited).
* Expected SNR: matched-filter SNR of the injected model on the star's own detrended, 30-minute-binned, un-injected curve (sigma = 1.4826 x MAD per bin), so gaps, noise and detrending are the real ones.

## 5. How to read these numbers

* Completeness is measured only inside the injected grid (1-40 d, Rp/R* 0.01-0.12, b < 0.9) on this sample; the survey searches to 100 d, and nothing here says what happens between 40 and 100 d.
* The SNR axis is the matched-filter SNR on this pipeline's own detrended, binned data, which is the quantity the packet quotes. It is not the SNR a different detrending would give.
* `alias` recoveries would be caught by a human looking at the folded curve at 2P or P/2; they are not credited because the automated ranking would carry the wrong period.
* Injections into stars that already carry a real or systematic signal compete with it for the detection slots; that competition is part of the measured completeness by design.
