**Why it is likely real**: 15 events over five years at one period with
odd/even depths equal to 0.2 sigma (8 odd, 8 even events from the template);
no secondary at phase 0.5 (measured 0.04 ppt against a planetary bound of
1.56 ppt); TRICERATOPS with 71 TIC stars within 10 px and the Tmag-sized
aperture gives FPP 0.0000 and NFPP 0.0000 (18 scenarios); no known object on
or within 21 arcsec of the star; the matched-filter SNR of 62.9 is in the
regime where the P2 completeness curve is 81% complete, so the search finding
it is unremarkable.

**What would kill it**: (1) The centroid: 0.009 px (0.2 arcsec) at 27 sigma
over 18 sectors, well above the 3-8 sigma the P0 module produced on known
planets in P3 and above the 4-15 sigma of the real blends there; if a
difference-image analysis on the SPOC 2-minute pixels confirms an offset, the
source is a neighbour (nearest TIC stars: Tmag 19.9 at 30 arcsec, Tmag 16.9
at 31 arcsec, which would need a total eclipse to make 2 ppt in this
aperture). (2) Per-unit depths disagree at more than 3 sigma across observing
units (flag), and the 12-h box rail says the events are broad on the
30-minute stitch: broad, shallow dips of unequal depth are what scattered
light and the chunked detrending produce at long periods. (3) The unconstrained
fit: until T14 and b are measured on the 2-minute data, Rp is anywhere from
4 to 30 R_Earth.

**What would settle it**: re-fit on the SPOC 2-minute light curves alone
(sectors 14-19 hold two or three events) to measure T14 and b, and inspect
those events one by one in the SAP flux; a difference-image centroid on the
2-minute target pixel files; a ground-based full-transit observation is hard
(one chance every 65 d, ephemeris uncertainty 0.06 d after the refit, 5-7 h
long, 2 ppt deep: a 1-m class telescope with an NEB check of the 31-arcsec
neighbour); high-resolution imaging to exclude companions inside 1 arcsec;
radial velocities would reach only a giant (a 5-6 R_Earth planet at 65 d
gives K of order 2-3 m/s).
